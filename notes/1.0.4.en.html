Added

  • Collections can have a color, shown on the header of their cards in the Library
  • Text, rich text, links and colors in the Library and Clipboard can be edited
  • New collections can be created from the Library menu
  • New text can be added to the Library from its menu or the management window

Changed

  • Cards in the Library no longer show the source app icon
  • Items pinned to the Library are sorted and dated by when they were added
  • A renamed item now shows its new name in the card header

Fixed

  • Links copied before title fetching was enabled are no longer fetched after pasting or pinning
